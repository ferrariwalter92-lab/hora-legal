Account Deletion Request

App Name: HORA

HORA allows users to request the deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Contents

  1. How to delete your account
  2. Data that will be deleted
  3. Data retention

How to delete your account

  • Open the HORA app
  • Go to Profile → Settings
  • Tap on "Delete Account"
  • Confirm your request

Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by contacting:
privacy@hora-app.com

Data that will be deleted

  • Phone number (if any), sign-in data and account information, including the identifier and email received from Apple or Google
  • If you signed in with Apple or Google: the access you gave HORA is revoked
  • Username and profile data
  • Connections with other users
  • Activity status and availability data

Data retention

Your data is deleted from our active systems immediately. Backup copies are deleted within 30 days. Reports made by you or about you are kept for 12 months to handle abuse, as explained in the Privacy Policy.
