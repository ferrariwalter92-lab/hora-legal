What it isn’t

Not for meeting people.

Hora is for people you already know. Everything else stays out.

  • No strangers.

    Only friends you added, and who added you.

  • No map.

    Hora doesn’t know where you are, and doesn’t ask.

  • No chat.

    When it’s Hora, you text where you already text.

  • No ads.

    No ads, no tracking. Your data is not for sale.
