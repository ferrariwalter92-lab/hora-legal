1. What HORA is

HORA is a free app that lets you know when your friends are free and helps you meet. It works between people who already know each other. It is not for meeting strangers.

The service is provided by Walter Ferrari, Viale Tibaldi 14, 20136 Milano, Italy. Contact: privacy@hora-app.com.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 14 years old and use your own Apple or Google account or phone number. One account per person. When you sign up, you confirm you are at least 14.

3. Your account

  • You sign up with Apple, with Google or with your phone number, then choose a username and the name your friends see. With a phone number you sign in with a code sent by SMS.
  • You are responsible for what you do with your account.
  • You can delete it at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your data is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Using HORA well

Do not use HORA to:

  • harass, threaten or make other people uncomfortable;
  • pretend to be someone else or use someone else's number;
  • collect other users' data or use automated systems;
  • do anything illegal.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable or abusive content and behavior. This includes names, usernames, bios and profile photos. If you break these rules, we can suspend or close your account.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. See our Child Safety Standards.

5. Block and report

If someone bothers you, you can block them: they can no longer send you a friend request or see when you are free. You can also report them from the app.

You can also decline a friend request: for 30 days the same person cannot send it again, and they are not told they were declined. Each person can send at most 30 friend requests a day.

We read reports within 24 hours. If needed, we remove the content and close the account of whoever broke the rules.

6. What you share

  • When you mark yourself free, you choose who knows it and until when (1 hour, 3 hours or “Tonight”, until 2 am), every time.
  • Your phone number or Instagram reach a friend only after a mutual “It's Hora!” (you both picked each other) and only if you chose to share them.
  • What a friend does with your contacts after “It's Hora!” (for example, messaging you on WhatsApp) is not up to us.

We are not responsible for meetings between users or for what happens when you meet. To the extent allowed by law, we are not liable for indirect damages.

We may change or remove features of the app. If we change these terms in an important way, we will tell you in the app.
